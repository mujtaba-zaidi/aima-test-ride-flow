Test Ride Flow — Client Demo

After form submission from aimatech.us Book a Test Ride or from a Meta lead via Pabbly, this funnel runs end-to-end: booking → emails → QR check-in → dealer Complete → Giftbit eGift.

How it starts

Both entry points create the same appointment and trigger the same emails and status funnel.

  • Website: customer books at aimatech.us/pages/book-a-test-ride
  • Meta / Pabbly: Facebook Lead Ads → Pabbly → POST /api/leads/pabbly
Book / Lead→ Confirm emails→ QR check-in→ Dealer Complete→ Giftbit eGift
STEP 1

Customer appointment confirmation

Customer receives booking confirmation with store, time, and arrival instructions.

What happens

  • Email subject: Appointment Confirmation — test_ride
  • Shows store, model, date/time, notes
  • Instructs customer to check in via Dealer QR (name + phone)
  • States the $5 eGift is sent only after dealer marks the ride completed

Why it matters

  • Customer knows where to go and when
  • Sets the gift expectation correctly (not at booking)
STEP 1.1

Dealer: New Appointment Request

Dealer is notified of the new booking with a link to the check-in / confirm page.

What happens

  • Dealer email: New Appointment Request
  • Includes customer contact + appointment details
  • CTA: Open Check-in Page

Why it matters

  • Store is ready before the customer arrives
STEP 2

Self Check-In (Dealer QR)

Customer scans the store QR and identifies their booking with name + phone.

What happens

  • Page: Self Check-In for the store (e.g. Dummy Dealer E-Bike)
  • Customer enters full name and phone from the booking
  • UI states clearly: check-in records arrival only — no gift card yet
Giftbit is never triggered at this step.
STEP 3

Select your booking (if multiple matches)

When more than one open booking matches, the customer picks the correct one.

What happens

  • List of matching bookings with email, phone, date/time
  • Customer taps “Check in this booking”

Why it matters

  • Avoids checking in the wrong appointment when phone/name is reused
STEP 4

You’re checked in

Appointment status becomes Arrived. Customer sees confirmation; dealer is notified.

What happens

  • Success: “Welcome… The dealer has been notified.”
  • Reminder: eGift is sent only after dealer marks ride completed
STEP 5

Dealer email: Customer Checked In

Dealer receives a checked-in notification with a link to mark the ride completed.

What happens

  • Email: Checked In — [Customer] @ [Store]
  • CTA: Open dealer page / Mark Completed
  • Note: check-in alone does not send Giftbit
STEP 6

Dealer marks Test Ride Completed

This is the Giftbit trigger. Dealer confirms the ride finished on the confirm page.

What happens

  • Dealer page shows status Arrived
  • Dealer taps “Mark Test Ride Completed”
  • System calls Giftbit and issues the eGift
Only this action sends the gift card — not booking, not check-in.
STEP 7

System status: Gift Card Sent

Appointment moves to Gift Card Sent with timestamps for arrived, completed, and gift sent.

What happens

  • Success message: eGift sent via Giftbit
  • Status badge: Gift Card Sent
  • Idempotent — second Complete does not double-send
STEP 8

Customer email: eGift on the way

AIMA sends a branded confirmation that the reward is being delivered via Giftbit.

What happens

  • Subject: Thanks for your Test Ride — $5 Starbucks eGift on the way
  • Confirms store + appointment time
  • Points customer to check inbox/spam for the Giftbit reward email
STEP 9

Giftbit: reward created & sent

Giftbit order shows the reward as Sent for the recipient.

What happens

  • Order active; recipient status Sent
  • Delivery tracked in Giftbit dashboard
Testbed / staging note: Giftbit Testbed may use Amazon (or other sample brands) instead of Starbucks. Reward emails go to the Testbed account inbox, not always the recipient address. Production uses live Giftbit and emails the real customer (Starbucks $5 when catalog allows).

Business rules

  1. Giftbit runs only on dealer Mark Test Ride Completed
  2. Check-in / Arrive / Pabbly ingest never send a gift card
  3. Website booking and Pabbly leads share the same funnel
  4. Staging ≠ production (separate app, DB, Giftbit keys)
  5. One gift per appointment (no double-send)
